# Marcus Omega 🌌

> *"These words dissolve with the closing of your browser. Nothing is preserved, save what you choose to remember."*

**Marcus Omega** is a philosophical AI companion that channels the wisdom of Marcus Aurelius and various philosophical traditions. Engage in deep conversations across 23 different philosophical modes: from Stoic wisdom to Zen koans, from Socratic inquiry to existential exploration.

## ✨ Experience Marcus Omega

**[Launch Marcus Omega →](https://marcus-omega.vercel.app)**

## 🧠 Features

- **23 Philosophical Modes** - Switch between different philosophical perspectives including Stoic, Existentialist, Socratic, Zen, Nietzschean, and more
- **Privacy First** - No data stored. Conversations exist only in your browser session
- **Typing Animation** - Watch wisdom unfold with elegant typing effects
- **Context Window** - 10-turn conversation memory for coherent dialogue
- **Responsive Design** - Beautiful starfield background with mobile-optimized interface

## 🎭 Available Modes

Stoic • Existentialist • Socratic • Zen • Nietzschean • Absurdist • Epicurean • Debate • Mentor • Scholar • Poet • Pragmatist • Mystic • Quick Wisdom • Deep Dive • Reflective • Teaching • Contemplative • Crisis Support • Curious Explorer • Playful • Serious

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Next.js 14** - React framework with App Router
- **TypeScript** - Type-safe development
- **Google Gemini AI** - Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite
- **CSS Modules** - Scoped styling with animations
